Stefano Casirati

I am a Postdoctoral researcher at UC Berkeley. My research lies at the intersection of ecohydrology, computational hydrology, and Earth system modeling. I investigate how land-use change and human activities, such as irrigation, shape hydrologic processes and land–atmosphere interactions across spatial and temporal scales. By combining statistical and geospatial analyses with process-based models and diverse data sources, including field observations and remote sensing, I explore the feedback linking vegetation dynamics, water fluxes, and climate variability. Before joining UC Berkeley, I earned my Ph.D. in Environmental Systems at UC Merced, where I examined how climate stressors and forest treatments influence mountain hydrology and forest ecosystems.
